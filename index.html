Questions

How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag, press Checkout, enter your details and send the pre-filled message on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm your order.

Do you deliver outside Krishna District?

Yes. We send parcels by courier anywhere in India for a flat ₹100 per order. Local delivery in Peddaparupudi Mandal is free above ₹799.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery for local orders. For courier orders, pay by UPI or bank transfer before dispatch. We share the details when we confirm your order. There is no online card payment on this site.

Do you ship outside India?

Not at the moment. Message us on WhatsApp if you have a special request.
